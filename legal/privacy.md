# Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 18, 2026

This policy explains what data this website (the "Service") collects and why.

## What we collect

**Account data.** If you create an account: a username and a password. Your password is never stored directly. It is run through a one way hashing function before it touches the database, so we cannot see or recover your actual password, and neither can anyone who gets access to the database.

**Chat messages.** Messages you send in the chatroom are stored so the chat history works. Chat messages are visible to other users of the Service by design; treat the chatroom as public.

**IP addresses.** Like any website running on a server, this Service can see the IP address your requests come from. This happens automatically as part of how the web works, whether or not an account is involved. Because this Service includes a proxy, your traffic to the sites you visit through it also passes through this server.

**Analytics.** We use Google Analytics to see how people use this site, things like page views and general usage patterns. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects data like your IP address and device information. That data is handled by Google under their own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## What we don't do

We do not sell your data. We do not share your data with third parties except Google Analytics as described above, or if required by law.

## How long we keep data

Account data and chat history are kept until you delete your account or ask us to delete it. You can request deletion at any time by emailing us.

## Cookies

If you log in, a session cookie is used to keep you logged in. It is not used for tracking or advertising. Google Analytics also sets its own cookies, described above.

## Children

This Service is not directed at children under 13, and you should not use it if you are under 13.

## Your choices

You can delete your account, or ask us to delete your data, by emailing us. We will remove your account data and chat messages tied to your account within a reasonable time.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated at any time. Continued use of the Service after a change means you accept the updated policy.
